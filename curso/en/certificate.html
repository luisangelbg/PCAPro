Certificate template

This is a template, not an official document The valid format, the institutional coat of arms, the reference numbers and the authorised signatures are provided by the Office of Continuing Education of the UACh. Before issuing any certificate, request the official format and the course registration number. Use this template only as a draft for agreeing on the wording and the details that must appear.

What must be checked before issuing

  • Registration of the course with Continuing Education and the reference number assigned.
  • Minimum attendance of 80 % (4 of 5 sessions) documented on a signed register.
  • Final mark ≥ 70 for a certificate with accreditation; below that, a certificate of attendance.
  • The participant's full name exactly as it appears on their official identification.
  • Authorised signatures according to the university's current procedure.

Syllabus content for course registration

Continuing Education usually asks for the syllabus broken down with hours. Here is the text ready to paste into the registration form:

UnitContentHours
1Foundations of principal component analysis. Origin and aims. The algebra of the method: eigenvalues and eigenvectors, scores and loadings. Structure of a data matrix. Loading data and declaring variable types.4
2Assumptions of PCA. Sampling adequacy tests: Bartlett's test of sphericity, the KMO index and MSA. Multicollinearity and the determinant. Handling missing data. Transformations and scaling: covariances versus correlations. Detecting multivariate outliers.4
3Component extraction. Explained and cumulative variance. Retention criteria: Kaiser–Guttman, Jolliffe, scree plot, Horn's parallel analysis, broken stick and Velicer's MAP. Justifying the decision.4
4Factor rotation. Thurstone's simple structure. Orthogonal rotations (varimax, quartimax, equamax) and oblique ones (promax, oblimin). Factor maps: correlation circle, map of individuals and biplot. Quality of representation (cos²) and contributions. Ellipses.4
5Interpreting and naming the axes. Supplementary elements and test values. Comparing groups on the components and the limits of doing so. Goodness of fit and residuals. Writing up methods and results. Generating the report. Integrated project.4
Total20

Further details usually requested

Mode of deliveryIn person, hands-on in a computer laboratory
Intended forPostgraduate students, researchers and professionals in the agricultural, forestry, biological and environmental sciences who work with multivariate data
PrerequisitesA knowledge of descriptive statistics and basic inferential statistics. No programming experience is required
Suggested group size12 to 20 participants
RequirementsA computer laboratory with Windows and the Chrome or Edge browser; a projector. Neither an internet connection nor the installation of licensed software is required
Material providedParticipant manual, five practice data sets and the PCAPro platform with a licence of use for the participant
AssessmentVerified practicals 30 %, examination 25 %, integrated project 45 %. Accreditation with 80 % attendance and a mark ≥ 70
